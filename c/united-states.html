Is B2B cold email legal in United States?

Yes, without prior consent when you write to a named person at a company. The same applies to generic addresses such as info@.

Named person at a company
jane.doe@company.com
No consent needed
Generic company address
info@company.com
No consent needed
Sole trader or personal mailbox
jane@gmail.com
No consent needed

No prior consent is needed. CAN-SPAM is an opt-out law and it covers business-to-business email as well.

What you have to do

  • Accurate From, To and Reply-To; a subject line that matches the content
  • Say clearly that the message is an ad
  • Include a valid physical postal address
  • Explain how to opt out and honour opt-outs within 10 business days

State laws are not covered here.
