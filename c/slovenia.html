Is B2B cold email legal in Slovenia?

Only in specific cases when you write to a named person at a company. For a generic address such as info@: yes, without prior consent.

Article 226 of the Electronic Communications Act (ZEKom-2) requires prior consent for marketing email, but paragraph 4 limits that to subscribers who are natural persons. Paragraph 6 allows email to a person's address when the company has published it as its own contact address. Every email must show who it is from and give a valid address for asking you to stop.

What you have to do

  • Write to a named employee only if the company published that address as its contact address
  • Do not hide or disguise who the email is from
  • Give a valid address where the recipient can ask you to stop
  • Existing customers: allowed for your own similar products if they could refuse free of charge at collection and in every message

The source is the official but unofficially consolidated text on PISRS, in use from 19 June 2025. The binding text is in the Official Gazette (Uradni list RS 130/22 and later amendments).
