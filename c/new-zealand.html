Is B2B cold email legal in New Zealand?

Only in specific cases when you write to a named person at a company. The same applies to generic addresses such as info@.

Section 9 of the Unsolicited Electronic Messages Act 2007 bans commercial email without consent. Consent can be express, inferred from an existing relationship, or deemed: when the address was conspicuously published by a person in a business or official capacity, without a statement refusing unsolicited messages, and the email is relevant to that person's business, role or duties.

What you have to do

  • Use only an address published openly for business, with no 'no unsolicited messages' note, and make the email relevant to the person's role
  • Identify who authorised the email and give contact details that stay valid for at least 30 days
  • Include a clear, free unsubscribe that works by replying the same way, and honour it within 5 working days
  • Do not use harvested address lists

The rules mirror Australia's Spam Act. Regulations can add or exclude circumstances for inferred consent; they are not covered here.
