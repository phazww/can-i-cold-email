Is B2B cold email legal in Belgium?

No, not without prior consent when you write to a named person at a company. For a generic address such as info@: yes, without prior consent.

Named person at a company
jane.doe@company.be
Consent first
Generic company address
info@company.be
No consent needed
Sole trader or personal mailbox
jane@gmail.com
Consent first

Advertising by email needs prior consent. According to Belgian law-firm commentary, a Royal Decree of 4 April 2003 exempts emails to legal persons at impersonal addresses such as info@.

What you have to do

  • Without consent, use only impersonal company addresses (info@, contact@)
  • Explain how to object and offer a working way to do so in every message
