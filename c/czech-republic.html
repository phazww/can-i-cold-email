Is B2B cold email legal in Czech Republic?

No, not without prior consent when you write to a named person at a company. The same applies to generic addresses such as info@.

Named person at a company
jane.doe@company.cz
Consent first
Generic company address
info@company.cz
Consent first
Sole trader or personal mailbox
jane@gmail.com
Consent first

Section 7(2) of Act 480/2004 allows electronic contact details to be used for commercial communications only for users who gave prior consent. The Act defines a user as any natural or legal person, so companies are covered.

What you have to do

  • Get consent before the first email
  • Mark every message clearly as a commercial communication, do not hide the sender, and include a valid address for refusing further messages (section 7(4))
  • Existing customers only: allowed for your own similar products or services if they can clearly refuse, free of charge, at collection and in every message (section 7(3))

No position of the data protection office (ÚOOÚ) specifically on emails to company addresses was found.
