Is B2B cold email legal in Switzerland?

No, not without prior consent when you write to a named person at a company. The same applies to generic addresses such as info@.

Named person at a company
jane.doe@company.ch
Consent first
Generic company address
info@company.ch
Consent first
Sole trader or personal mailbox
jane@gmail.com
Consent first

Article 3(1)(o) of the Unfair Competition Act treats mass advertising by email as unfair unless the sender has prior consent, names the correct sender and offers a free, easy way to refuse. Whether business recipients are treated differently was not confirmed.

What you have to do

  • Get consent before automated mass mailings
  • Existing customers only: exception for your own similar offers if they were told they could refuse

Based on Swiss legal publishers, not the SECO page or the statute text. The rule targets mass advertising.
