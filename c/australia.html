Is B2B cold email legal in Australia?

Only in specific cases when you write to a named person at a company. The same applies to generic addresses such as info@.

Consent first

The Spam Act requires consent, express or inferred. Consent can be inferred when a work-related address is conspicuously published, carries no statement that commercial messages are unwanted, and your message relates directly to the recipient's role.

What you have to do

  • Check that the address is published openly with no "no commercial messages" statement
  • Make sure the message relates directly to the recipient's role or function
  • Identify the sender and include a working unsubscribe
