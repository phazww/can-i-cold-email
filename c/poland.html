Is B2B cold email legal in Poland?

No, not without prior consent when you write to a named person at a company. The same applies to generic addresses such as info@.

Named person at a company
jane.doe@company.pl
Consent first
Generic company address
info@company.pl
Consent first
Sole trader or personal mailbox
jane@gmail.com
Consent first

Article 398 of the Electronic Communications Law bans sending commercial information, including direct marketing, to a subscriber or end user without prior consent. Polish legal commentary reads this as covering companies as well as individuals.

What you have to do

  • Get consent before the first email, tied to the specific address
  • Do not treat a published address as consent

No UKE or UODO statement on B2B email was found. The B2B reading comes from commentary.
