Consent first

CASL requires consent, express or implied, for commercial email, including B2B. Consent is implied when the recipient has conspicuously published the address without a statement that they do not want unsolicited messages, and your message is relevant to their business or role.

What you have to do

  • Check that the address is published openly and carries no "no solicitation" statement
  • Make sure the message relates to the recipient's business, role or duties
  • Identify yourself and include a working unsubscribe mechanism

A personal mailbox is treated here as needing consent unless one of the implied-consent cases clearly applies.
